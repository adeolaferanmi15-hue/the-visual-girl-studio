07 / Good to know

A few things
you might wonder.

Is the content entirely AI-generated?

Yes. Models, environments, styling, product scenes, designs and video elements are generated using AI, with creative direction and quality control led by The Visual Girl.

Do packages include revisions?

Yes. Every package and individual service includes two rounds of revisions. Additional rounds or changes beyond the agreed brief may attract an extra fee.

Can I request something outside these packages?

Absolutely. Custom projects are welcome. Share your brief and the deliverables you need, and a tailored quote can be prepared.

Can I use the content on my brand platforms?

Usage and delivery formats will be agreed as part of each project. Any extended usage or additional adaptations can be discussed when preparing your quote.

How do I get started?

Choose a package or individual service, then send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email with a little information about your brand and requirements.
